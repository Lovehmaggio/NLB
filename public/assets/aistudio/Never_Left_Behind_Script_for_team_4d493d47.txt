Never Left Behind
Revised 3-Minute Pitch Script
Act I: When the World Went Silent
Presenter 1 | 0:00–0:45

[The stage is dark. Hold three seconds of silence.]

Before the water came, there was darkness.

At 2:00 in the morning, Hurricane Maria tore through Utuado, Puerto Rico. Mateo, who was deaf, sat beside his grandmother, whose oxygen machine depended on electricity.

He could not hear the wind tearing through the mountains.

But he felt the concrete trembling beneath him.

Then he watched the green light on her oxygen machine flicker...

once...

twice...

and disappear.

His phone had no signal. Without light, he could not read lips or see anyone sign.

Then water touched his feet.

And one thought consumed him:

Does anyone know we are here?

The storm did not make Mateo invisible.

The system had failed to see him long before the rain began.

Act II: A System That Understands the Person
Presenter 2 | 0:45–1:35

[The Never Left Behind interface appears.]

That is why we created Never Left Behind, an NLP-powered accessibility and evacuation-readiness system designed to understand the person behind the emergency.

Instead of forcing someone in crisis to navigate different agencies, databases, and inaccessible forms, the system allows them to describe their situation in plain language:

“I am deaf and use a wheelchair. My grandmother needs oxygen. There are three people in our party, and we do not have a vehicle.”

From that sentence, the engine identifies the information responders need:

The user’s location and contact information
Disabilities and communication needs
Mobility and travel limitations
Medical or equipment dependencies
The number of people in the party
Transportation and shelter requirements

The user does not need to understand emergency terminology.

They only need to explain what is happening.

The system does the work of understanding what survival requires.

Act III: Turning Information Into a Way Out
Presenter 2 | 1:35–2:25

Once the user’s needs are understood, Never Left Behind locates them and cross-references their situation against three connected knowledge areas.

First, it searches nearby healthcare facilities for resources capable of supporting medical needs, including oxygen dependency and continuous electrical power.

Second, it evaluates available emergency shelters, looking beyond distance to determine whether a location can accommodate wheelchairs, medical equipment, the full party, and specific accessibility needs.

Third, it reviews public transportation and paratransit options to determine how the family can physically reach safety.

The system then combines those findings with route and hazard information.

Because the closest shelter is not always the safest shelter.

A building may be nearby but have stairs.

A facility may be open but lack backup power.

A bus route may exist but be inaccessible to a wheelchair.

And a road that appears passable may lead directly through rising water.

Never Left Behind evaluates the entire journey, not just the destination.

Act IV: From Data to Coordinated Action
Presenter 2 | 2:25–2:45

In seconds, scattered information becomes one personalized evacuation plan:

A verified destination.

An accessible route.

The transportation required.

The medical and accessibility accommodations needed upon arrival.

And the contact information authorized responders need to reach the family.

When information is missing, the system does not guess.

It identifies the gap, flags the risk, and prioritizes options that can be verified.

Act V: No More Names Lost in the Dark
Presenter 1 | 2:45–3:00

Survival should not belong only to those who can hear the siren, run down the stairs, drive themselves to safety, or breathe without electricity.

Never Left Behind turns one sentence into recognition, recognition into action, and action into a way out.

Because no one should spend their final moments wondering whether the world knows they exist.

And when the lights go out...

no one should be left behind.

Why This Balance Works
Act I retains the emotional intensity, but reaches the problem statement within 45 seconds.
The system is explained as a clear sequence: understand the person → locate resources → evaluate accessibility → create a coordinated plan.
The script demonstrates that NLP is not merely collecting words. It is translating human circumstances into specific operational requirements.
Concrete examples, such as stairs, backup power, wheelchair transportation, and flooded routes, make the system’s intelligence easier to understand.
The ending returns to Mateo’s central fear: not whether information exists, but whether the system recognizes that he exists.
